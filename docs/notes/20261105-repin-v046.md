## 4. Lessons

- A raw sweep in the hundreds of thousands was one rule on two corpora. Count by
  (corpus, rule) before reading it as breadth.
- A new `literal-string` message in the reference cannot be mirrored without the type;
  declining keeps parity on the (rule, line, col) key and on the message.
