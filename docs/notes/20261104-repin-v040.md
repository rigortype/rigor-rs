## 2. Raw bump: 1 fixture FP + 2 sweep FPs, two upstream commits

- **F1: `900bc64c` (#1359, the `$_` frame-local work).**
  - **Upstream change:** `eval_loop`'s single body pass now enters on the predicate's
    loop-entry edge, and the body's end scope joins the exit.
  - **The FP:** after `while h.is_a?(Unknown)`, `h` is no longer `Array`, which retracts
    fixture 100's `b31b`.
  - **A second FP, older than this bump:** after `until h.is_a?(Unknown)` the port fired and
    neither reference pin did. The exit of `until` is the predicate's truthy edge.
  - **Port fix:** a new `loop_edges.rs` with an allow-list of predicate shapes (truthiness,
    `nil?`, class guards, `===`, `!`, `&&`/`||`), and `Node::Loop` gains `LoopKind`. Both
    edges are overlaid in `check_env_at`, `collection_shape` and `flow_eval_scope`.
- **F2 + F3: `1be2fd0b` (#1453).**
  - **Upstream change:** guards now narrow constant receivers, keyed by spelling, so
    `::File` and `File` are different keys. `respond_to?(:m)`'s truthy edge reads
    `Dynamic[top]` when the receiver's class lacks `m`.
  - **The FPs:** mail's `if ::File.respond_to?(:makedirs); ::File.makedirs …` and
    concurrent-ruby's `old_value.respond_to?(:nan?) && old_value.nan?` under
    `kind_of? Numeric`.
  - **Port fix:** a suppression-only walk, `guard_suppression.rs`, feeds the rules
    driver's skip set.
  - **Attribution:** `git bisect` blamed F3 on `c1f96e24` (#1344), but the implementer's
    per-commit re-run showed `1be2fd0b`.

**Result:** 0 FP on fixtures and sweep, and MATCHED counts are identical to the raw bump on
all 8 corpora. One coverage loss is known: after `while h.empty?`, the reference fires
`for non-empty-array[top]` and the port is now silent.
